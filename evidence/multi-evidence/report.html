What changed

Raw OCR and existing reading-direction alternatives are compared with nearby OSM names and historical aliases, plus the MOI natural-feature gazetteer. Native 20 m DTM measures relief and elevation at modern coordinates. A 1 km search accommodates displaced labels without snapping them to a summit.

Names, numeric agreement and proximity remain distinct. Missing evidence means unknown. Saved annotations and OSM links are used only for evaluation. Existing detections, display priorities, coordinates and annotation state are preserved.

Decision: land explainable counterpart ranking and this review queue. Defer automatic display promotion: numeric identity, historical units and independent accuracy are not established. Hollow-circle filtering remains rejected; the legends include meaningful trees and circular landmarks.

Controlled comparisons

Distance-only ordering puts the saved OSM counterpart first in 1/12 cases. Automatic text/type evidence puts it first in 4/12 and in the first three in 8/12, versus 4/12. None ranks lower. These 12 associations represent only 9 distinct OSM objects; they are selected development examples, not 12 independent trials or a detection recall estimate.

The gazetteer adds 3 name leads (拳頭母山 and two detections of 阿玉山). Terrain provides measured elevation/relief, but adds no demonstrated ranking improvement in this sample. OSM and the gazetteer may share source material and are never counted as independent votes.

Of 103 saved classifications, both name-supported/ambiguous cases are POIs; all 46 Noise and 34 Other cases remain undecided. This small set has no annotated symbol POI positives and cannot establish safety of symbol suppression.

Visual check and rejected experiment

All 16 surfaced crops were inspected after the matching rules were fixed. Fourteen were not previously annotated; several overlap known locations or duplicate the same mark, so this is a visual spot check, not a blind geographic holdout. The crops show actual names or numbers, but that alone does not verify which modern object they identify. The first digit in #8028 is uncertain and illustrates why OCR/height agreement must not become automatic confirmation.

A separate four-rotation crop OCR trial on the 103 annotated marks recovered several contour numbers but did not recover the important unread peak/elevation labels reliably. It also produced partial and wrong numbers. Rejected for production; its readings do not feed this comparison.
