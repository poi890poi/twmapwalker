Use Find a name to search the current map view and selected historical series. Open a finding to edit its reading. Selecting a possible name only fills a draft; Save reading stores a separate revision. A complete reading can be explicitly marked verified; a reading containing ? must remain tentative. Finding confirmation and raw OCR are separate.

Suggestions use your query and nearby local complete readings, including clearly labeled OCR candidates. They do not consult an external gazetteer or prove historical identity. Search ranks before pagination and applies to export. Very broad searches disclose their evaluation limit.
