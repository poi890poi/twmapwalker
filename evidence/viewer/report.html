Search, filters, sort and direct page navigation

Text + “With a name”, sorted by name, 25 per page. Page two and the filters survive reload. “With a name” means a stored OCR or manual reading exists; it does not mean that reading is correct.

A cluster click moved from zoom 15 to zoom 17. The trail filter then showed 11 trail candidates; ungrouped trails retain their line geometry. Purple lines are detector proposals, not verified walkable routes.

OSM as a layer and structured supporting evidence

Actual search for 溪, with OSM at 70% opacity. Blue geometry shows 64 nearby OSM features from mountain-oriented categories within 1 km of the map center. OSM features are separate from historical POI counts.

The historical finding’s panel shows the OSM record for 桶後溪, its original tags, and an approximate 378 m separation. It also returns 加九寮步道 and other nearby features. This is contextual support, not proof that the historical mark denotes the modern feature.

Structured context is fetched through a separate persistent background queue, with one request at a time, caching, failure backoff, query versions, retained raw responses and SHA-256 hashes. Raster OSM tiles are loaded directly by the browser for interactive viewing only. The historical exclusion mask and detector versions are unchanged.
