Rules and measured selection

Version display-1 ranks raw automatic text and shape evidence, then selects in fixed geographic cells corresponding to 128 pixels at the current zoom. Top: priority floor 0.74, at most 1 per cell. Reduced: 0.50, at most 5. Adaptive: 0.38, at most 2. Adaptive permits weaker proposals in sparse cells but does not force low-signal noise into empty areas. Complete cells are ranked before clipping to the viewport. Zooming reveals more. The scores are not probabilities.

Tile loading: what is and is not downloaded

Before this change, E: contained 821 complete downloaded tiles across the three sources, mainly Wulai. Taiwan-wide imagery was not fully downloaded. Different zoom levels use different tiles.

A controlled slow upstream download demonstrated a shared-lock defect: an unrelated cached hit waited 512.86 ms behind a 500 ms delay. The viewer now reads published immutable cached tiles independently, retaining SHA-256 verification. The regression proves a cached hit completes while the upstream request remains blocked. New downloads retain their existing throttle and serialization; cold views, server request-pool contention and tunnel/network delay are not eliminated.

The exact Wulai phone viewport is now cached at zooms 5–16 for both historical sources and NLSC: 189 tiles verified, 50 newly downloaded, zero failures. No new detection jobs were queued. Native historical zoom ends at 16. NLSC zooms 17–19 and uncached areas remain on demand. No bulk OSM raster download was performed. A warm 28-tile local HTTP batch with six concurrent requests took 285.4 ms; this is not a measurement of cellular or public-tunnel performance.

Decision and retained limitations

Land: display selection, historical trail deferral in normal browsing, and cached tile lock isolation. Set aside: trail detection quality work, based on the user's observations of widespread noise and missing real trails. Existing trail records and explicit legacy API access remain; no detector implementation or registry was changed. Unproven: landmark accuracy improvement, calibrated quality thresholds, complete Taiwan caching and public-network speedup. No fresh ground-truth holdout was collected for accuracy.

104 automated tests pass. Tests cover mode budgets, panning stability, unknown text, independence from manual labels, shared map/list/export membership, paging/sorts, trail exclusion and cached-read integrity. All rows in algorithms, tiles, jobs, attempts, POIs, reviews and readings match the immutable pre-change SQLite baseline. Existing detections were neither deleted nor rerun.
