Decision and next method

Retain the guarded crop verifier as a research baseline. Reject the context CRAFT candidate. Noise removal is not solved: the small safe gain is concentrated in vegetation, and the models do not handle rare landmark symbols reliably.

The next model needs separate contour, vegetation, marginal-print, text, landmark-symbol and unknown classes, with real hard negatives and rare-symbol positive training examples. Keep numerals and ambiguous marks protected. Test each class on separate map areas; allow the verifier to abstain. “Other” and “no OCR/OSM match” must not become automatic noise labels.

Detailed decision and acceptance gates. No application code, stored annotations or detector weights changed.
